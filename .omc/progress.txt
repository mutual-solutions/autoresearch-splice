## AGC experiment (discarded)
- AGC normalizes amplitude but phase is independent of amplitude
- At -20dB RMS, phase estimation is already accurate — AGC adds nothing
- At -40dB RMS (near silence), phase is noise-dominated — AGC can't fix that
- Conclusion: phase-based detection has a fundamental floor around -30dB
- Below that, need different approach (noise floor spectrum comparison, not phase)
